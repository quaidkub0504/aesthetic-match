Questions

Good to know.

Is this medical advice or a diagnosis?
No. Aesthetic Match produces an educational profile and a set of provider matches — never a diagnosis or an automated treatment plan. A licensed clinician determines what's appropriate for you.
How much does it cost me?
Discovery and matching are free for patients. Practices pay a subscription for the tools and measurement — so recommendations are never pay-to-win disguised as clinical fit.
What does "verified" actually mean?
In a real build, any "verified" badge is backed by an actual licensure and credential check, kept current — not a self-declared claim.
How is my privacy handled?
Privacy-by-design: we minimize what we collect, and we don't ask for medical histories or facial photos up front. A production version would address applicable HIPAA, FTC health-app, and state privacy requirements before launch.
Is paid placement a thing?
If it ever exists, it's disclosed distinctly and never disguised as clinical suitability. Matches are driven by credentials, services, preferences, and proximity first.
Is this a live product?
This is a product concept by Alteva Digital, proposed for a pilot in the Howell, Michigan area. Figures, timelines, and profiles shown here are illustrative examples, not commitments.
